# 7.77 Program — Digital Productivity Presentation Curriculum
**Topic:** Digital Productivity: Work Smarter. Save Time. Reduce Stress.  
**Audience:** Adults & Professionals (Ages 31–45)  
**Format:** Online Interactive Workshop (60 minutes)  
**Facilitator:** Abderrahmane SAOUDI (Tech Lead, Creative Engineer & Former GDG Ghardaia President)  
**Platform:** `saoudi.online/777`

## 15-Slide Master Curriculum Map

| # | Slide Title | Type & Interaction | Primary Objective | Allocated Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Digital Productivity** | Keynote Cover (Looping Video, 7.77 Branding) | Set tone, welcome attendees, establish official national initiative context. | 2–3 min |
| **02** | **About Your Facilitator** | Facilitator Profile & 3 Experience Pillars | Build immediate credibility through UI/UX, software development, and GDG community leadership. | 2 min |
| **03** | **What's Your Biggest Time-Waster?** | Interactive Poll (1–5 Click/Keyboard select + Chat call) | Engage audience early; diagnose shared workplace digital bottlenecks. | 3–4 min |
| **04** | **A Typical Busy Day** | Relatable Overload Grid + End-of-Day Question | Illustrate the illusion of busyness vs. actual finished progress. | 3–4 min |
| **05** | **What Is Digital Productivity?** | Core Definition + 4-Quadrant Conceptual Model | Define intentional technology use: Less Friction, Less Repetition, Better Focus, More Output. | 3 min |
| **06** | **Before Installing Another App...** | 4-Filter Decision Framework (Eliminate, Simplify, Template, Automate) | Inoculate against "shiny tool syndrome"; fix the workflow before adding software. | 3 min |
| **07** | **Build a Simple Productivity System** | Interactive 5-Step Workflow (Capture → Organize → Prioritize → Execute → Review) | Establish the conceptual spine of the workshop: 1 system beats 5 disconnected apps. | 3 min |
| **08** | **Stop Relying on Memory** | Practical Sequence (Tasks = WHAT, Calendar = WHEN) + Live Audience Exercise | Concrete walkthrough of "Report by Thursday" using Google Tasks and Calendar time-blocking. | 10 min |
| **09** | **AI as Your Everyday Assistant** | 6 Concrete Use Cases + Crucial Data Privacy Guard | Demystify AI as a daily drafting accelerator while setting strict verification/privacy boundaries. | 4–5 min |
| **10** | **From Chaos to Clarity** | Interactive Prompt Comparison + Live [Copy Prompt] + Step-by-Step Table Reveal | Show real difference between weak and structured prompts with zero hallucination. | 5–6 min |
| **11** | **The Better Workday** | Interactive Before & After Reveal (Hotkey `A` / Toggle Button) | Contrast reactive mental fatigue with structured control and peace of mind. | 4 min |
| **12** | **Your Minimal Productivity Toolbox** | 5 Functional Categories (Communication, Files, Tasks, Docs, AI) | Prove that existing standard tools are already enough without paid subscriptions. | 3–4 min |
| **13** | **Your 2-Minute Productivity Challenge** | 4 Guided Steps + Live 02:00 Countdown Timer + Chat Share | Active learning: Participants optimize one annoying recurring weekly task right now. | 9 min |
| **14** | **Questions & Discussion** | 4 Clickable Category Starters (Tools, AI, Teams, Organization) | Structured Q&A addressing real workplace hurdles with empathetic advice. | 8 min |
| **15** | **Thank You!** | Keynote Closing + Portfolio Link + Replay Button + Chat Pledge | Inspire one concrete improvement starting tomorrow; closing acknowledgments. | 3 min |

**Total Duration:** Exactly 60 minutes.
