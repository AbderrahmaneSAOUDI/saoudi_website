7.77 Digital Skills for Every Generation

A national digital empowerment initiative led by Algeria's Ministry of Post and Telecommunications, in partnership with Algérie Télécom and Mobilis. It aims to strengthen digital skills for citizens aged 7 to 77 through practical, inclusive, and accessible training in areas such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, coding, data, cloud technologies, and digital entrepreneurship.

7-77

Age Range Covered

Continuous digital learning pathways tailored from young students (7) to lifelong senior learners (77).

07

Skill Centers

Core specialized technology and training hubs deployed across wilayas to deliver local training.
